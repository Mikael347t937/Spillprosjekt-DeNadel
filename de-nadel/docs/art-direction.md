# Art direction

Background generated with the built-in imagegen tool from three user-provided references: the title-screen concept, the film moodboard, and the monumental city model. Reference files were not added to the repository. UI, title lettering and syringe mark are separate Godot/vector elements.

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed 16:9 background painting for a Godot title menu, 1920x1080 or wider landscape. References: image 1 is the primary composition and visual mood reference; image 2 is a supporting gritty dystopian art direction moodboard, not a layout to reproduce; image 3 is supporting monumental city architecture reference. Create a NEW original cinematic illustrated dystopian title-screen background, dark charcoal/ashen gray with dirty ivory highlights and limited deep crimson accents. A weary young resistance courier in a battered hooded coat dominates the left third in close-up, looking upward, realistic expressive face, inky graphic novel contour and rough painted texture. On the right half and distant upper center: a colossal authoritarian civic complex, stepped stone towers and distant huge dome, searchlights sweeping through smoky overcast sky, a large crimson banner with a FICTIONAL severe leader portrait, low catwalk guard silhouettes and a few exhausted figures at bottom right. Strong atmosphere, rain haze, smoky layers, restrained embers. Keep center from x=42% to x=68%, y=25% to 90% very dark and low detail, suitable for a live title and vertically stacked menu buttons. Match image 1's cinematic illustrative quality and close character composition, with architecture informed by reference 3. The actual text/buttons will be rendered by the game engine. Absolutely no text, no lettering, no logo, no UI, no watermark, no collage panels, no real-world party symbols. Keep the image full bleed without borders.

Saved project asset: `assets/art/title_background.png`.

Fonts: Anton and Barlow Condensed, distributed under the SIL Open Font License; license files are in `assets/fonts/`. Ambient drone and click audio were synthesized specifically for this project.
